🧠 How does this math work?

Every app charges roughly: base fare + (₹/km × km) + (₹/min × min), with a minimum floor, times a surge multiplier during rush hour.

  1. Finding places: ⭐ popular places come from data.js instantly. 📍 Any other address is looked up on OpenStreetMap (free), which gives us its latitude/longitude.
  2. Distance: the "haversine" formula gives straight-line km between A and B, then we multiply by a detour factor (ROAD_FACTOR, roads bend).
  3. Time: we guess city traffic speed from the distance (~12–26 km/h).
  4. Rate card: the ₹ numbers in data.js. Metered autos add a night charge between midnight and 5 AM.
  5. Learning: every real price you log is compared with what the raw formula predicted (surge removed). With 2 or more logs for the same app+vehicle, we compute how wrong the formula usually is and quietly correct future estimates.
